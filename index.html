EPFL spin-off project · Lausanne

Infrared sensors that count single photons at room temperature

Redstart Technologies designs sensors that detect single photons of short-wave infrared light: containing an array of pixels, working at room temperature. We are building them into a camera for life-science imaging and into receivers for quantum communication.
